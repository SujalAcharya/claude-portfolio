My story

Patience, practice and good timing.

It started with a scrambled cube and a stubborn streak. I joined the Bhanubhakta Cube Association, learned to solve it, then to solve it faster, and in 2019 I entered my first official WCA competition.

When I moved on to Xavier International College, I found the same feeling somewhere new: a drum kit. Rhythm is just another puzzle you solve with your hands. In 2021 I joined three friends to form EFGH, a rock band, and we have been playing shows ever since.

Now I study BSc CSIT at Patan Multiple Campus, where code ties it together: practice, patterns and a lot of patience. Great things take time.
